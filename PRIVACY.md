# Privacy Policy: LeetCode Sync

LeetCode Sync does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There is no server, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the extension accesses

- **On leetcode.com:** it reads the code, language and verdict of your own submissions when you click Submit, plus the problem's number, title and difficulty. It doesn't read anything else on the page.
- **Your GitHub token and repository name:** entered by you on the settings page and stored locally in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`). They never leave your device except as part of requests to GitHub.

## Where data goes

Your accepted solutions are sent **only** to `api.github.com`, into the repository you chose, using your token. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes the stored token and settings. You can also revoke the token at any time in your GitHub settings.

## Contact

Questions? Open an issue on this extension's GitHub repository.
